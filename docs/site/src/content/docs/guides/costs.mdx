**Roughly USD 200–250 a month while you are building, and EUR 10–20 a month to
run.** Almost all of the first number is the AI subscription, which is the one
thing you can turn down the month you stop building; almost all of the second is
one rented server, which you cannot. There is no per-seat price, no per-record
price, and nothing that grows when your team does — adding the eleventh person
to your app costs nothing.

<Aside type="tip" title="The one recommendation">
  **Claude Max 20x, USD 200 a month**, for the weeks you are actively building. Pro at USD 20 is
  enough to try the kit and make small changes. A wrong turn costs more than a stronger plan, which
  is the whole argument.
</Aside>

## Two habits that keep the bill where it should be

**Raise the effort before you change the model.** The same model can be told to
think harder, and that is the cheaper lever. The agent will say when it genuinely
needs a stronger one; a session cannot switch itself, so that is a request for
you.

**Keep to one conversation at a time.** Running several agent threads in
parallel uses your weekly allowance several times faster. One at a time, with at
most a couple of helpers, normally stays comfortably inside it.
