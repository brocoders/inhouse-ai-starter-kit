You describe what your company needs. An AI coding agent builds it on
foundations that are already decided, tested and written down. You try it on
your phone the same afternoon, and one command puts it on your own server.

If you lead something and you are the one who ends up fixing the process, this
is for you.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="One place that is always current" icon="document">
    Orders, cash, clients, members, equipment — whatever your part of the business actually runs on,
    in a system that fits it rather than nearly fits it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Used standing up, on a phone" icon="laptop">
    Every app installs on the home screen, works offline enough to be pleasant, and is designed at
    phone width before desktop width.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Yours, on your own server" icon="rocket">
    One rented computer, one command to release, a copy of the database every night. No seat prices,
    no export deadline, no vendor.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Written down for a leader to read" icon="approve-check">
    Every decision, release and incident is recorded in plain words. Months later a new conversation
    already knows the project.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
